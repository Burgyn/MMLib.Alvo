Alvo's descriptor covers most backend logic: rules, computed fields, rollups, before- and after-hooks. When it cannot
express something, an embedded host writes C# — and that code joins the **same write pipeline, events and guarantees**
the descriptor's hooks use.

Start with [Where logic goes](/extending/where-logic-goes/): the first question is not *how*, but *where*.

## What exists in this build

The host-extensibility line is complete: every page below describes something that runs. They fall into six groups.

### Where logic goes, and how it is called

- [Where logic goes](/extending/where-logic-goes/) — descriptor, CEL function, hook, handler or command: which one fits.
- [Custom CEL functions](/guides/custom-cel-functions/) — pure, synchronous, in the write's transaction.
- [Call Alvo from your endpoints](/guides/call-from-endpoints/) — `IAlvoData` and `IAlvoEvents.PublishAsync`.
- [Typed or CEL](/extending/typed-or-cel/) — handlers over your own classes, conditions as lambdas, typed reads.
- [Generated types](/extending/generated-types/) — typed classes, enums, patches and role constants the build
  generates from your descriptor or a Management API snapshot.
- [Commands and inbound webhooks](/extending/commands/) — named, access-ruled actions over HTTP or in process; Standard Webhooks received verified and once.
- [HTTP conventions](/extending/http-conventions/) — Alvo's caller on your own endpoints, conventions on one entity's routes, and the gate that stays on.

### Writes

- [Validate and guard writes](/extending/write-hooks/) — C# in a write: validate before its transaction, guard and
  complete inside it, with the caller's rules applied to everything a hook does.
- [Transactions in your own code](/extending/transactions/) — several writes, batches and events as one unit, with savepoints and a time limit.
- [Integrity in the descriptor](/extending/integrity/) — a field's `validation` as a `CHECK` the database enforces, a
  rollup that counts some children, and time with `now()` and durations.
- [Rules over relations](/extending/relation-rules/) — a rule that follows a ref or reaches the records that point
  here, always through the related entity's own list rule; lookup checks against a related row.
- [State machines and timers](/extending/state-machines/) — the values a status field may move between, as named
  transitions with roles and a condition; per-record timers armed, moved and cancelled in the write's transaction.

### Events and deliveries

- [The event envelope](/extending/event-envelope/) — what every event carries: who acted, in which tenant and flow,
  and which fields it leaves out.
- [React to changes (event handlers)](/extending/event-handlers/) — C# after a write commits: at least once, retried,
  in order per record.
- [Multi-step side effects](/extending/steps/) — named steps that each complete once, sleeping, debouncing and
  concurrency limits.
- [Functions in the descriptor](/extending/functions/) — a C# function registered with `AddFunction`, called from an
  after-hook or an automation rule.
- [Automation](/extending/automation/) — event and cron rules in the descriptor, each action its own retried delivery.
- [Operate deliveries](/extending/operate-deliveries/) — history, pause, redeliver, replay and fixtures for your handlers.

### Time

- [Time: timers and schedules](/extending/time/) — a keyed timer that fires once at its latest time, a cron schedule
  that runs once across replicas, a data migration that runs once per revision.

### Reads

- [Shape what reads return](/extending/reads/) — narrow which rows a caller reads, and hide a field or fill a virtual
  one in every returned row, after the rules.

### Seeing, checking and testing

- [See your extensions in the dashboard](/extending/extension-catalog/) — the catalog, the Lifecycle tab, and the apply
  guard.
- [Analyzer rules](/extending/analyzer-rules/) — `ALVO1001`, shipped inside `MMLib.Alvo`: what it reports in hooks and
  CEL functions that run inside the write's transaction, and what it cannot see.
- [Testing your backend](/extending/testing/) — `MMLib.Alvo.TestHost`: your descriptor through the real pipeline,
  inside a unit test, with event delivery on your schedule.

## The line is complete

Nothing in the host-extensibility line is still to come. The design, and the history of how it was built, is
[host extensibility](https://github.com/Burgyn/MMLib.Alvo/issues/370); a few follow-ups are open there, and the
[Roadmap](/project/roadmap/) lists what this build still does not run.

## One limit already enforced

Every write and every published event counts how deep it sits in one causation chain. Past
[`Alvo:Extensions:MaxDepth`](/reference/configuration/#alvoextensions) (default 8) it is refused with
[`508 chain-depth-exceeded`](/reference/problem-types/#chain-depth-exceeded), which is never worth retrying.
